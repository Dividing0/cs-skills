# Threat Model Systems: result template

## Result

State the verified outcome, concrete design or supported diagnosis. Label unresolved claims.

## Contract and assumptions

Record inputs, output semantics, model, versions, platform, workload/resource/failure assumptions and missing evidence relevant to this task.

## Analysis or change

Describe the implementation/model, invariants, tradeoffs and code/artifact references. Preserve a witness or counterexample where applicable.

## Validation

Record command/method, input/seed, tool versions, outcome, scope/bounds and raw evidence location. Separate proof, bounded checks, tests, simulation and measurements.

## Limits and acceptance

List acceptance criteria met, unknowns, unsupported cases and the smallest remaining checks needed for the claimed deployment or guarantee.
